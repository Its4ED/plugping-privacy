Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

PlugPing is developed by Made by 4ED / its4ED. PlugPing is designed to provide charging and battery-related utilities while respecting user privacy.

Information PlugPing accesses

PlugPing may access information required for its core functionality, such as:

  • Battery level
  • Charging status
  • Charging connection and disconnection state
  • User-selected PlugPing settings and preferences
  • Notification functionality required to provide alerts

This information is processed locally on the user's device.

Data collection

PlugPing does not collect or transmit personal information to Made by 4ED.

PlugPing does not:

  • Require an account or login
  • Collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or addresses
  • Collect precise location
  • Collect contacts, photos, messages, or files
  • Use advertising SDKs
  • Use analytics or behavioural tracking
  • Sell user information
  • Share user information with advertisers or other third parties

Data storage

Preferences necessary for PlugPing to operate may be stored locally on the user's device. Made by 4ED does not operate a server that stores PlugPing user data.

Users can remove locally stored PlugPing data by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

Data sharing

PlugPing does not share personal or sensitive user information with third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Because PlugPing does not transmit user data to Made by 4ED servers, Made by 4ED does not retain personal user information.

Locally stored application information can be deleted by clearing PlugPing's app data or uninstalling PlugPing.

Children's privacy

PlugPing does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Security

PlugPing processes its core application information locally on the device and does not transmit personal information to Made by 4ED servers.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PlugPing's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will remain available at the Privacy Policy URL listed on Google Play.
